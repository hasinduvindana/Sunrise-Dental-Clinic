Need Assistance?

Frequently Asked Questions & Help

Find answers to common questions about booking sessions, payments, and clinic procedures.

How do I book an appointment through the website? ▼

You can click on the "Get Appointment" button or select a slot directly from our Doctor Schedules section. Enter your NIC, Name, and Phone number to receive your official Appointment Booking Slip with your Token Number.

Can I pay for my appointment online? ▼

Online payment is not collected during booking. All consultation charges and treatment fees are settled at our Cashier Reception desk upon your arrival using Cash or Credit/Debit Card (Visa, MasterCard, AMEX).

How do I access and print my diagnostic reports? ▼

Navigate to the "Patient Reports" section, enter your NIC number, and press Enter. You will be able to view and download all verified X-rays, CBCT scans, and treatment summaries formatted as standardized PDF documents.

What should I do in case of a dental emergency? ▼

For acute dental emergencies such as trauma, severe facial swelling, or uncontrolled toothache, please call our 24/7 Emergency Dental Hotline at +94 77 123 4567 or visit our clinic directly.
